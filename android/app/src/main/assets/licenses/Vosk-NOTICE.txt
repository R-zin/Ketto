Kettoo offline speech demo uses the unmodified Vosk engine by Alpha Cephei
and the unmodified vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15 English acoustic model.

Engine source: https://github.com/alphacep/vosk-api
Model source and Apache 2.0 listing: https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models
License: Vosk-Apache-2.0.txt in this directory.

Recognition is automatic. The original audio remains available for review.
